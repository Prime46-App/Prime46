## Scope
BODY / THE VESSEL only. v0.1 understands Push, Pull, Squat, Carry, Cardio, Mobility, and Balance. Work Capacity and Body Control remain outside the implementation until their specifications are locked.

## Non-negotiable invariants
1. Blocked actions never become PRIME-prescribed.
2. Missing evidence never causes invented capability or invented loads.
3. Training never silently becomes verified Capability.
4. Historical bests never replace current verified reality.
5. Very recent exposure can suppress redundant demand without deleting underlying Need.
6. Player rejection never deletes Need.
7. Behavioral preference is not motive.
8. Explicit goals may affect Need through goal relevance without erasing weaknesses.
9. Boss readiness is not created by time alone.
10. Worse Boss results never lower an undefeated Boss target.
11. Verified Boss/Assessment evidence outranks prior inference.
12. Insufficient evidence produces RE-ESTABLISH rather than guessing.
13. No valid candidates is a legal engine result.
14. CHOOSE never manufactures alternatives to satisfy a quota.
15. Recommendation provenance remains explicit.
16. MODIFY does not inherently reduce priority.

## CARDIO Special-Role Rule
Cardio may operate as a PRIMARY QUEST TARGET, a COMPANION LAYER, or ACTIVE RECOVERY. Recency applies to the specific Cardio demand, intensity, and purpose—not to Cardio participation as a whole. Active-recovery Cardio remains Training data but does not automatically count as meaningful developmental Cardio exposure.

## PRIORITIZE implementation position
v0.1 uses qualitative rule ordering, not additive numeric weights. The engine returns reason codes and observable signals instead of a pseudo-precise priority score.
