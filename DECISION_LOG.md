## 2026-09-09 — LOCKED — HOW PRIME THINKS v0.1
- Canonical decision pipeline: FILTER → PRIORITIZE → PRESCRIBE → BOSS CHECK → CHOOSE.
- FILTER: What is appropriate and realistically possible right now?
- PRIORITIZE: What would create the most valuable development right now?
- PRESCRIBE: What should today's version of that selected challenge actually be?
- BOSS CHECK: Has enough changed that it is worth proving?
- CHOOSE: Given PRIME's judgment, what will the player commit to doing?
- Governing principle: REALITY constrains → DEVELOPMENT prioritizes → ADAPTATION personalizes → AGENCY decides.
- Constraints and Opportunity are gates. Need, Evidence, Balance, and Recency are relative priority signals rather than a rigid numeric hierarchy.

## 2026-09-09 — LOCKED — FILTER v0.1
- Core question: What is appropriate and realistically possible right now?
- FILTER determines eligibility, not priority. Only eligible actions reach PRIORITIZE.
- Four gates: LIMITATIONS · OPPORTUNITY · EVIDENCE / ENTRY · CURRENT APPROPRIATENESS.
- Outputs: PASS ✓ · MODIFY ↔ · BLOCK ✕.
- Governing principles: PRIME DOESN'T RANK WHAT SHOULDN'T BE DONE. PRIME protects the player without trapping the player in old information.
- Hard constraints are gates, not negative weights.
- Preserve the developmental target by modifying method when appropriate before eliminating the target.
- Significant limitations can block or modify a Quest regardless of developmental Need.
- Opportunity constrains what can actually be prescribed today.
- Missing evidence limits prescription specificity; it does not automatically prevent play.
- PRIME does not invent starting capability or workload to overcome missing evidence.
- Recent evidence can make a challenge inappropriate today without pretending PRIME knows biological recovery merely from recency.
- If a Quest fails as written but a valid alternative method exists, PRIME may return the target to PRESCRIBE for modification.
- If no appropriate Quest survives, PRIME may truthfully return no recommendation rather than fabricate one.
- Player agency does not require PRIME to endorse an action it considers inappropriate; player-initiated and PRIME-prescribed actions remain distinguishable.
- Constraints are evidence-based states, not permanent labels.
- Constraint lifecycle: ACTIVE → CLEARING → CLEARED. A reappearing limitation can move CLEARING back to ACTIVE.
- New relevant evidence can weaken, clear, or reactivate a constraint.
- Constraint persistence reflects severity, repetition, freshness, specificity, and contradictory evidence rather than arbitrary timers.
- Significant or repeated limitations generally require stronger contradictory evidence to clear than isolated minor limitations.
- Apply limitations at the narrowest scope justified by evidence; a method-specific problem does not automatically block an entire capability/domain.
- If a prior constraint is too stale to treat confidently as current, PRIME RE-ESTABLISHES rather than assuming either persistence or resolution.
- A player's report that a limitation resolved is meaningful evidence but does not automatically prove unrestricted capability under the previous challenge.
- Successful relevant performance without the limitation is stronger clearing evidence than declaration alone.

## 2026-09-09 — LOCKED — PRIORITIZE v0.1
- Core question: What would create the most valuable development right now?
- PRIME ranks eligible actions by their combined developmental case using NEED · EVIDENCE · BALANCE · RECENCY.
- NEED: Where would development create the most leverage? Includes limiter severity, weaknesses, maintenance needs, advancement opportunities, and goal relevance.
- EVIDENCE: What important uncertainty would be valuable to resolve? Includes missing/stale evidence, confidence, and information value.
- BALANCE: How appropriately distributed has meaningful developmental exposure been? Detects neglect, underexposure, and overrepresentation without imposing a rigid weekly split.
- RECENCY: How recently did this specific capability receive meaningful exposure? It can temporarily suppress unnecessary repetition without erasing Need.
- Major limiters outrank ordinary improvements.
- Important unknowns can outrank known weaknesses when information value is high.
- Information value determines which unknowns matter most; missing evidence is not automatically urgent.
- Neglect raises priority but does not automatically overpower developmental Need.
- Very recent meaningful exposure suppresses unnecessary repetition without erasing Need.
- Established strengths tend toward maintenance unless advancement is intentionally valuable.
- Important unresolved evidence can gain practical urgency rather than remaining unknown forever.
- Repeated player refusal does not cause PRIME to pretend underlying Need disappeared.
- No single signal automatically wins every conflict.
- New verified evidence outranks old inference.
- PRIME explains the strongest reasons behind a recommendation.
- When no correction is strongly needed, PRIME shifts from fixing weaknesses toward expanding capability and pursuing the next meaningful breakthrough.
- Player goals, priorities, and seasons affect goal relevance inside Need; they are not a separate top-level PRIORITIZE signal.

## 2026-09-09 — LOCKED — PRESCRIBE v0.1
- Core question: What should today's version of the selected challenge actually be?
- Architecture: ANCHOR = Current Evidence · TRAJECTORY = Training History · RESPONSE = Adaptation · SAFEGUARD = Limitations & Staleness.
- Current verified evidence anchors demonstrated Capability. Historical capability remains context/history, not current prescription authority.
- Training History establishes the dose trajectory and can advance prescription without silently advancing Capability.
- ADAPT inputs: EFFORT = EASY / RIGHT / HARD; LIMITATION = NONE / MINOR / SIGNIFICANT; CAPACITY REMAINING = MORE / ABOUT RIGHT / EMPTY.
- Objective performance constrains contradictory subjective Adaptation.
- Significant limitations can override ordinary progression.
- Staleness reduces evidence authority for current prescription.
- When current evidence and useful training history are insufficient, PRIME RE-ESTABLISHES rather than guesses.
- Dose decisions: PROGRESS ↑ · CONSOLIDATE → · MODIFY ↔ · REGRESS ↓ · RE-ESTABLISH ⟳.
- Quest output contract: TARGET · METHOD · DOSE · INTENT · SUCCESS CONDITION.
- Overarching rule: PRIME NEVER INVENTS CAPABILITY TO JUSTIFY PRESCRIPTION.
- Current verified evidence anchors demonstrated Capability.
- Training performance may advance prescription without silently advancing Capability.
- Objective Training History establishes dose trajectory; Adaptation interprets the player's response.
- One non-critical difficult response normally informs rather than automatically reverses a successful trajectory.
- Repeated non-critical response patterns carry more authority than isolated non-critical responses.
- Historical PRs remain history and do not override current demonstrated reality.
- Training develops Capability; verified Assessment/Boss performance proves Capability.
- Progression should remain controlled and interpretable; one primary demand progresses at a time by default.
- MODIFY changes method/configuration while preserving target where possible. REGRESS reduces dose while generally preserving method. RE-ESTABLISH represents insufficient current knowledge, not failure.

## 2026-09-09 — LOCKED — BOSS CHECK v0.1
- Core question: Has enough changed that it is worth proving?
- Core philosophy: THE BOSS DOESN'T ASK WHETHER YOU TRAINED. IT ASKS WHETHER YOU CHANGED. REALITY GETS THE FINAL VOTE.
- Evaluate RELEVANT DEVELOPMENT · DEVELOPMENTAL TRAJECTORY · TRANSFER EVIDENCE · EVIDENCE FRESHNESS · CURRENT APPROPRIATENESS.
- Boss states: WAITING → REMATCH AVAILABLE → REMATCH RECOMMENDED.
- Developmental Readiness determines availability; Today Readiness determines whether PRIME recommends fighting now.
- Boss model separates BENCHMARK · CURRENT VERIFIED RESULT · UNDEFEATED BOSS TARGET.
- Regression updates current reality without lowering the undefeated Boss target.
- Boss cycle: WAITING → evidence accumulates → REMATCH AVAILABLE → stronger case/current appropriateness → REMATCH RECOMMENDED → BOSS FIGHT → VERDICT → READINESS RESET → NEW DEVELOPMENTAL CYCLE.
- A legitimate Boss attempt consumes/resets the developmental evidence that justified the rematch; another PRIME-recommended rematch requires new post-attempt development.
- Repeated standardized Boss results that contradict training-predicted improvement reduce confidence that the current training trajectory is transferring and can feed PRESCRIBE toward MODIFY without inventing a cause.
- Indirect training improvement never overrides direct standardized evidence for the tested capability.
- Time alone does not create Boss Readiness. Training completion alone does not create Boss Readiness.
- Boss evidence must be relevant to the capability tested.
- A Boss can be developmentally ready while still inappropriate today.
- Significant limitations can suppress Boss recommendation.
- Stale evidence creates uncertainty; it does not imply improvement.
- Player refusal does not erase Boss Readiness.
- Boss performance is verified evidence and outranks prior inference.
- Any legitimate improvement defeats the Boss; no arbitrary minimum improvement threshold.
- Equal = STALEMATE. Worse = BOSS SURVIVES.
- A defeated Boss becomes history; the new verified result becomes the basis of the future YOU, LAST TIME.
- Player-initiated Boss attempts and PRIME-recommended Boss attempts remain distinguishable; player agency is final subject to FILTER.

## 2026-09-09 — LOCKED — CHOOSE v0.1
- Core question: Given PRIME's judgment, what will the player commit to doing?
- Core philosophy: PRIME GIVES DIRECTION WITHOUT TAKING CONTROL.
- Supporting principle: THE PLAYER CAN TEACH PRIME WHAT MATTERS—BUT REALITY STILL DETERMINES WHAT WORKS.
- Default choice architecture: 1 PRIME RECOMMENDED Quest · up to 2 PRIME-ranked alternatives · subordinate Browse BODY Activities escape hatch.
- PRIME recommends; the player chooses.
- CHOOSE does not rerun PRIORITIZE or flatten its rankings.
- The strongest eligible developmental action receives PRIME RECOMMENDED status.
- Alternatives must have legitimate developmental value; they are not filler and remain visibly subordinate to the recommendation.
- PRIME presents a small curated choice set rather than the entire eligible action space.
- The default is one recommendation and up to two ranked alternatives; PRIME does not manufacture options to satisfy a quota.
- Browse remains available as a subordinate escape hatch for broader agency.
- Rejecting PRIME's recommendation carries no punishment by itself.
- Player refusal does not cause PRIME to pretend the underlying developmental Need disappeared.
- FILTER remains authoritative; CHOOSE cannot convert a blocked action into a PRIME-prescribed Quest.
- Player-initiated actions remain distinguishable from PRIME-recommended and PRIME-ranked actions.
- Recommendation explanations surface the strongest observable reasons rather than fake precision or hidden weighting.
- Selecting a Quest creates commitment and moves the player from CHOOSE into ACT.
- Changing a choice before meaningful action begins is not failure or abandonment.
- Once meaningful action occurs, PRIME preserves what actually happened rather than rewriting session history.
- Boss recommendations enter CHOOSE through the same agency principle: PRIME may recommend the rematch; the player decides whether to fight.
- CHOOSE preserves freedom without manufacturing equivalence.
- Repeated choices are evidence of behavioral preference, not proof of motive or developmental value.
- Persistent mismatch between player choices and PRIME recommendations should trigger clarification rather than inferred intent.
- Explicit goals, priorities, and seasons can legitimately alter goal relevance inside PRIORITIZE without erasing known weaknesses/constraints.
- Successful player-directed choices that produce verified progress become evidence for future PRESCRIBE/recommendation decisions.
- PRIME may adapt how strongly/frequently it presents a persistent recommendation without pretending the underlying Need disappeared.
- PRIME does not force balanced development; it makes the consequences and tradeoffs of imbalance visible.
- Developmental Priority and Player Priority are distinct truths and may disagree.
- Player Intent is goal context inside Need, not a new flat top-level PRIORITIZE signal.
